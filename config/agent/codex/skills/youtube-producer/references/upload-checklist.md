# YouTube upload and discovery

Reviewed 2026-11-07 against official YouTube guidance. Recheck changing controls and account eligibility when performing an upload. This is guidance, not proof that an account has a feature or that an upload passed checks.

## Minimal upload handoff

Show only the fields relevant to this video, in this order:

1. Local Google Drive To Upload path for the final video and approved thumbnail; Notion card link.
2. One selected title and one final description, with hashtags inside the description only.
3. Applicable playlist and category, audience choice, age restriction when warranted, AI-use disclosure when applicable, paid-promotion disclosure when applicable, and language/caption review.
4. Review processing and available copyright/ad checks; choose visibility or the exact scheduled date/time/time zone with Nathanial. Do not invent a schedule or claim checks guarantee rights clearance.

The title limit is 100 characters and description limit is 5,000. Uploading, finishing processing and going public are separate events. [Official upload flow](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57407?hl=en).

Keep the closed project/media backup in Drive Editing and final upload assets in To Upload. Verify local checksums and remote file presence/sizes before calling the handoff complete. Do not describe a local CloudStorage copy alone as synced. Save actual paths/links and verification level on the Notion card. This backup order is Nathanial's workflow, not a YouTube requirement.

## What stays optional

- Tags-field list: omit unless asked. YouTube still supports tags, but says their usual discovery role is small. [Tags](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402?hl=en).
- Separate keyword or hashtag sections: omit from upload notes. Use relevant phrases naturally; put three relevant hashtags at the end of the description by default. Three is our preference, not a required number. [Hashtags](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en).
- Chapters, end screens, cards, pinned comments and social posts: include only when useful to the video or requested. Do not invent chapter times or force chapters into a single-song lyric video.
- PDFs/ZIP handoffs and extra title variants: only when requested or a meaningful decision needs them.
- Caption tracks: recommend accurate captions for accessibility; do not assume auto-captions correctly recover rap or that burned-in lyrics replace a selectable caption track. Only supply reviewed files that exist.

## Disclosure and audience

AI-generated music that is the video's focus needs the Studio AI-use disclosure. A text disclosure in the description is not a substitute. Ordinary production assistance is different from generated music. [AI-use guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en).

Made for kids and 18+ age restriction are separate decisions. Assess the actual video; do not age-restrict all music automatically. Repeated strong profanity and adult content resembling family content may warrant restriction, while artistic context matters. [Age restriction](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2802167?hl=en), [language policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10072685?hl=en).

Use the approved thumbnail; verify dimensions, file size and available controls for the current format/account before promising upload compatibility. Do not substitute another candidate. [Thumbnail guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en).

## Discovery decisions that matter

- Lead titles with the song, topic or viewer payoff. Keep them concise and accurate. Nathanial prefers no creator/artist name in titles by default; use it only when it adds necessary identification or he requests it. YouTube recommends keeping important words first and branding/episode numbers later. Removing a name is a preference, not a proven ranking boost. [Titles and thumbnails](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300?hl=en).
- Put the specific video promise and one or two natural topic phrases in the first description lines, before the shared Wolf Den footer. Keep each video's opening unique. No keyword dumps, unrelated character/show names, inflated claims or invented search-volume data. [Descriptions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449?hl=en).
- Search relevance includes the title, description and actual content, alongside engagement and quality signals. Do not promise ranking from hashtags or a metadata score. [Search](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16090438?hl=en).
- Build one clear thumbnail promise and deliver it early in the video. After publication, use available Analytics to assess search terms, impressions/CTR by traffic source and retention; interpret them together rather than optimizing clicks alone. Recommendations also reflect what viewers watch and enjoy, not merely metadata. Do not invent universal CTR/retention targets or claim measured improvement without data. [Recommendations](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16533387?hl=en).

Preserve the approved thumbnail and original song; metadata research does not authorize re-editing the video or replacing approved art.
