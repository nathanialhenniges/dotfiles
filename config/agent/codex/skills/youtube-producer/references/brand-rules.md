## Identity and exact artwork

- Spell **MrDemonWolf** exactly. Use **MrDemonWolf, Inc.** for the company. Write spoken scripts in Nathanial's natural first-person voice, with concrete claims and light wolf humor only when it fits. Keep Kommit and client identities separate.
- Reuse approved original logos and character art. Never generate a replacement logo, recolor, redraw, stretch, rotate, crop or add effects/accessories to an official mark. Uniform scaling and placement are allowed. Leave at least one quarter of the visible artwork's height clear on every side. Do not mistake transparent padding for clear space.
- The bundled approved-transparent-wolf.png is character artwork, not proof that every official logo variant is supplied. The example thumbnail is composition inspiration, not episode content or an official logo.
- Keep the blue/cyan face and navy fur markings faithful to the selected reference. **Eyes stay black**, with the source's white sclera/reflections; do not change them to blue, gold or amber. When the hoodie is shown, it is **red with white drawstrings**. Do not invent a zipper on a pullover. Do not add, lose or fuse ears, paws, fingers, limbs or accessories. Preserve the selected reference's pose/anatomy; don't impose a generic animal paw count on existing anthropomorphic artwork.
- If generation changes character identity or anatomy, reject that candidate. Compose with the original art instead. No generic AI wolf, invented reaction face or redraw of the official character. Never manufacture real episode screenshots, UI, data or results.
- Preserve supplied asset bytes and embedded content credentials in the source package. A derived thumbnail must not be described as the untouched original or as retaining a verified signature without evidence.
- **Protected Twitch emotes must never enter AI tools**, including generation, vision, OCR or ChatGPT uploads. Do not use them as thumbnail references or skill assets. Other approved branding artwork is separate.

## Colors and type

Use the current YouTube/website palette: Brand Navy #0A1633, Wolf Smoke #0D2A56, Deep Blue #0D4D8C, Sky Blue #3AAEE3, Frost #90DDFC, Ice #EFF7FF and white #FFFFFF. The selected social lettering treatment #BFEAFF is also approved. Older #091533 and #0FACED/#00ACED are legacy/project-specific tokens, not automatic replacements for this palette.

Use Montserrat for headlines, Roboto for supporting copy and JetBrains Mono only for useful technical labels. Use bold headline weight when needed for thumbnails. Keep 2–4 clear words, placed with a text/composition tool for exact spelling. Finish the actual thumbnail with that text when the tool allows; otherwise identify the delivered art as textless and include the exact overlay copy as a remaining step.

Aim for **7:1 text contrast** against the actual background behind every headline. Prefer white/ice on a plain navy region, or navy on a pale region. A color swatch alone does not prove contrast across a photo/gradient; measure or inspect the relevant background. State when a numeric check is unavailable. Do not claim an entire thumbnail, YouTube page or image has AAA certification. Check readable subject scale and headline at a reduced preview size without requiring a physical-phone test.

## Required review before delivery

- Confirm exact character/logo identity, eyes, hoodie details, anatomy, aspect ratio, spelling and no unintended text.
- Confirm the thumbnail promise matches the script and title; no fake claims, inflated outcomes or fabricated screenshots.
- Compare the output against the original reference, not against the previous generated candidate. Check the finished image at full size and a reduced thumbnail preview. Reject/fix defects before calling it ready.
- Name any unmet condition plainly. Do not mark a prompt, diagram, missing image or uninspected render as a finished thumbnail.
