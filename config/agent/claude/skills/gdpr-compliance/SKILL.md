# gdpr-compliance

When the preserved workflow conflicts with this entrypoint or current user and environment rules, follow this entrypoint and the active rules.

Read workflow.md for audit and drafting procedures. Verify current law and regulator guidance from official sources; templates are starting points.
Trace actual personal-data flows, purposes, lawful bases, minimization, security, retention/deletion, processors, transfers, and rights handling. Distinguish implemented controls from intended policy.
Use references/privacy-notice.md, references/dpa-template.md, and references/documents.md for the requested document type.
Report evidence, gaps, impact, and concrete fixes. Do not guarantee compliance; identify decisions needing qualified legal review.
Review alone does not authorize data deletion, notices, agreements, or retention changes.
